What is real and what is game

  • Real: the PLC program greenhouse.st runs unmodified in an IEC 61131-3 interpreter, scan by scan (50 ms), in your browser. It is the same file that passes 114 automated tests and runs on OpenPLC Runtime v4.
  • Model: the greenhouse — weather, air temperature and humidity, crop water use, pump and dripper hydraulics, nutrient and pH chemistry — is a physics model. The PLC only sees it through its sensors, like a real plant.
  • Game layer: crops, varieties, health and harvest are added for this page, and their clock is accelerated: one simulated minute of good daylight counts as one growing day.

Project, documentation and tests: github.com/rayangmendrofa/horticulture-farm-plc.
