## Preconditions

- [ ] Panel built and inspected against drawings GH1-E-01...E-12; all wire ferrules and terminal markers fitted.
- [ ] Dead tests recorded: PE continuity (-X0 to every exposed conductive part), insulation resistance of the power circuits ≥ 1 MΩ at 500 V DC with electronics disconnected and the 0V-PE link at -X0 opened.
- [ ] Supply checked at -X1: 400 V between phases, 230 V L-N, phase rotation clockwise.
- [ ] Power-up in steps: -Q0 ON, -F1 ON, 24.0 V DC (±1 %) at -G1; then -F21...-F25 one at a time.
- [ ] OpenPLC Runtime v4 running `plc/greenhouse.st`; remote device PLANT pointing at -A1 192.168.10.20:502 (docs/openplc.md). HMI connected (E-14).
- [ ] Mechanical systems ready before any motor runs: pumps primed, fans guarded, vent and net drives free, acid circuit filled with water for the first runs.

Outputs are tested from the HMI in **IRR MANUAL** or **CLIM MANUAL**, so the PLC interlocks stay active (FDS §5.1). Only the stack light has no manual command: use the OpenPLC Editor debugger to force it, with nothing else forced. Remove every force at the end.
